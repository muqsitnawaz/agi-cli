- **Bound keychain-helper spawns with timeouts and a daemon reaper (RUSH-2231, RUSH-2232).**
  Every `keychain-helper` and `/usr/bin/security` `spawnSync` in `agents secrets`
  now carries a hard timeout (8s for silent verbs, 60s for interactive ones) and
  `SIGKILL`, so a wedged `coreauthd` / `LocalAuthentication` dialog can no longer
  hang the `agents` process forever. A new daemon reaper runs every 5 minutes and
  kills orphaned helpers (PPID 1, >30s) and stuck `agents` parents with a live
  helper child (>90s, child-first, then parent escalation). Source:
  `apps/cli/src/lib/secrets/index.ts`, `apps/cli/src/lib/secrets/reaper.ts`,
  `apps/cli/src/lib/daemon.ts`.
